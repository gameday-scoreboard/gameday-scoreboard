Support

Common fixes, and how to reach us for anything else.

The app can't find my panel

Add it by address instead. Open the panel's own page at gameday-xxxxxx.local (the name it shows on screen), or the numeric address underneath if your phone can't resolve .local names, and enter that in the app.

The panel won't join my Wi-Fi

The panel only joins 2.4 GHz networks. If your router has separate names for 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz, pick the 2.4 GHz one, and check the password. Use your main network: a guest network can let the panel join but keep your phone from reaching it.

No iPhone? Setup covers the panel's own hotspot.

Half the board is blank, or the picture shows twice

The panel layout doesn't match your panel. Open the panel's page, find Setup and click the picture that matches yours: One 128x64 panel for the panel we ship (or two 64x64 panels side by side), One 64x64 panel for a single small one. The panel restarts with the new layout.

I need to reset Wi-Fi

Hold the controller's boot button for 10 seconds. The screen warns you first, with 5 seconds to let go if you didn't mean to. Once it resets, the panel forgets its network and restarts into setup mode; your team, favorites and other settings stay as they were.

A shorter hold (about 1.5 seconds) just shows the panel's address again, without resetting anything.

Start over from scratch

Factory reset on the panel's page erases everything: Wi-Fi, team, favorites and settings. The panel restarts into setup the way it came out of the box.

Updating the firmware

The panel checks for updates every six hours on its own. To check right away, open its page and use Check for Updates; an Install button appears there when a newer version is out and the update runs over Wi-Fi.

The panel won't start

If it stays dark or keeps restarting, reinstall the firmware from the installer page, with a USB cable and a desktop browser such as Chrome or Edge. That erases its settings, so set it up again afterwards.

Something else

Email support@gameday-scoreboard.com. Say what the panel or the app was doing, and include the firmware version from the panel's page if you can.

Built your own and found a bug? GitHub issues work too: github.com/gameday-scoreboard/gameday-scoreboard/issues.
