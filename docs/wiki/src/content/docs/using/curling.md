Curling gets its own board, for a men's rink or a women's rink followed in [Favorite teams](/using/favorites/). This page describes a 128x64 display and notes where a single 64x64 panel differs. See [Panel sizes and layouts](/using/panel-sizes/).

Curling is the one sport here that does not come from ESPN, which has no curling at all. The panel reads the Grand Slam of Curling site's data feed (the Sportsnet curling data) instead: the same public feed the Slam's own site runs on, no account and no key.

## What it covers

The feed carries the season's events as one list, and the panel follows the event on now, or the next one to start:

- **The Grand Slam of Curling**, all season: the Masters, the National, the Open, the Players' Cup and the rest, men's and women's rinks at the same event.
- **The Brier** (men) and **the Scotties** (women) in their weeks.
- **The world championships and the Olympics**, in the years and weeks the feed carries them.

One event at a time: when two overlap, the panel follows the one that started later, and comes back to the other when it ends. A rink that is not at the event on now has no game until an event it plays in starts.

## Picking a rink

Follow a rink the way you follow any team: **Men's curling** and **Women's curling** are their own tabs in the team picker, with the rinks by skip's name (Dunstone, Homan, Mouat, Einarson and the rest of the field, including the rinks that only play the Brier or the Scotties).

The feed has no team logos, so the board draws the rink's three-letter code in place of a logo, on the panel and on the device page. There are no team colors either: splashes show in white.

## Before the match

The next-match card shows both codes with your rink's win-loss record from the event's standings under it. The ticker carries the event's name ("GSOC Masters", "Brier"), the start day and time in the panel's timezone, the draw ("Draw 14"), the stage ("Round Robin", "Quarter Final") and the arena.

Curling rinks have no Up next list: the standings name the next match, and that is the one the panel follows.

## During the match

- **The score and the end on now** under it, like "End 3". Curling has no game clock, so the end takes the clock's place.
- **The hammer**: the ticker says which rink throws last in the end on now, like "DUN hammer". The feed names the first end's hammer (the last stone first end, decided by the draw to the button before the match); each later end's goes to the rink that did not score the end before, and a blank end keeps it where it was.
- **A score splash** when an end lands: "SCORES 3!" for your rink, and the other rink's code with its points, like "HLU SCORES 2", unless you turn off **Opponent scores too** under **Celebrations** on the device page's Display tab. The tally only moves once an end, so a splash is an end finishing, never a single stone.

Grand Slam matches play eight ends; the Brier, the Scotties and the championships play ten. A match still level after the last end plays an **extra end**, and the clock row reads "Extra end". A tied round-robin match at a Grand Slam event is settled by a draw to the button instead, with no extra end played.

## The final

The ticker says "Final", "Final/EE" after an extra end, or "Final/SO" when a draw to the button settled it (the score stays level; the winner is the rink the splash names). In Favorite teams the final stays up for the **After the final** time (see [Favorite teams](/using/favorites/#when-a-game-is-on)).

At the final the board takes turns, 10 seconds each, with the line score: each rink's points by end and the total, the ends in one row of single figures on a 128-wide board, with an EE column after an extra end. On a 64x64 panel the rinks sit side by side under their codes, with the ends grouped by halves around the mid-game break.

## What curling does not have

The feed is a scores feed, not ESPN, so some cards and modes do not apply:

- **Live games** does not scan curling: there is no day's scoreboard of every match to pick the best one from. Follow a rink in Favorite teams.
- No **News headlines** and no **Game leaders** card: the feed has neither.
- Only your rink's record shows before a match; the feed's standings give the other's on their own page, not in the match.
- The playoffs are followed once the round robin ends: the panel walks the event's remaining matches for your rink's next one. A rink whose event is over has no game until the next event it plays in.
