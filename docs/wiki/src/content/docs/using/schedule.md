Between games the panel can show what's coming up for your team, and once the season is over, how it went.

Both cards are for your teams in **Favorite teams** mode in every league but MLB: football (NFL and college), soccer (MLS and the Premier League), basketball (NBA, WNBA and men's and women's college) and hockey (NHL). Baseball teams don't get them: an MLB team's season schedule is too big for the panel to read.

## Turn them on or off

Both are on to start. **Schedule** and **Season recap** are under **Between games** on the [device page](/using/device-page/). Without a team outside MLB in the playlist that's playing, they're grey and say so.

## What they show

Every 5 minutes between games, one of your favorites takes a turn, and the next turn is the next favorite's.

- **Schedule**, during the season: the team's next three games under a line like **DAL SCHEDULE**, each with the day, the date, the opponent (**@** for a road game) and the start time, plus the TV network when there's room. On two panels each game takes one line, like **Sun 11/1 vs ARI 12P Fox**; on one panel, two.
- **Season recap**, between seasons: the season's record under a line like **DAL 2025**, and how the playoffs ended, like **11-6, lost NFC Wild Card** or **15-2, won Super Bowl LX**. Soccer records are wins, draws and losses, and hockey records are wins, losses and overtime or shootout losses, like **43-33-6**. A college basketball team's tournament shows its round, like **lost NCAA Sweet 16**. A team that missed the playoffs shows its record alone.

A team is between seasons when the panel's [off-season clock](/using/brightness-and-clock/#the-off-season-clock) would come up for it: no game left on its schedule, or for soccer, nothing for more than 10 days, which can include a long international break.

Each card stays up for 10 seconds, longer if its lines roll, then the board comes back.

## Where it comes from

The panel reads each team's season schedule from ESPN: about 200 to 400 KB for a football or soccer team, and up to 2 MB for a basketball or hockey team (an NHL team's whole season), which takes the panel 10 to 20 seconds. It reads each football or soccer favorite at most once every 6 hours and each basketball or hockey favorite at most once a day, and only while one of the two cards is on and it's that team's turn. If a read fails, the panel tries that team again in 15 minutes (an hour for basketball and hockey) and goes on to the next one meanwhile. When a team's season ends, or its next one starts, the panel reads it again first (once its retry time has passed), so the recap includes the last game.

## When they step aside

Like the [news card](/using/news/), they never cover a live game, a celebration, or the panel's own trouble messages, and they take turns with the message card, the news card, the College Top 25 and your pictures: none of them comes up over another. The countdowns and the weather card are the board's own turns, so a schedule or recap card can come up over them, the same as a headline.
