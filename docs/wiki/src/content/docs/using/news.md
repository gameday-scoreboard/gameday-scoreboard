Between games the panel can show the latest sports headlines from ESPN, one at a time, so the board has something new to say while you wait for the next game.

## Turn it on

The news card is off to start. Turn on **News headlines** under **Between games** on the [device page](/using/device-page/).

## What it shows

Every 3 minutes between games, one headline takes the screen for at least 10 seconds, under a line that says whose news it is, like **DAL NEWS** or **NFL NEWS**. A headline too long for the screen rolls up slowly, then the board comes back. The next turn shows the next headline, six in all, then they start over.

Where the headlines come from follows the panel's mode:

- **Favorite teams**: your favorites' news, from the playlist that's playing. The panel reads one favorite's headlines, then the next favorite's an hour later, and so on down the list.
- **Live games**: the news of the leagues the panel follows, one league each hour.

The panel reads ESPN's news about once an hour, and only while the card is on. If a read fails, the headlines it already has stay, and it tries again in 10 minutes.

## When it steps aside

The news card works like the [message card](/using/messages/#a-message-card):

- It never covers a live game, a celebration, or the panel's own trouble messages, like **No internet**. It comes back on its own once they're gone.
- It and the message card take turns. Neither one comes up while the other is on the screen, and each waits 10 seconds after the other goes down. A message card on **Whenever no game is on** keeps the board, so the news waits.
- It doesn't come up over the [College Top 25](/using/top-25/) either, and the Top 25 waits while a headline is up.

Headlines are in English, as ESPN writes them. Emoji are left out, since the panel can't draw them.
