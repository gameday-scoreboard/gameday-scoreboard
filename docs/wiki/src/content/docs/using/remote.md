The panel works with a WiZ WizMote, the small battery remote sold for WiZ smart bulbs. It talks to the panel directly, with no hub and no app, and works whether or not your phone is nearby.

## Pairing

1. Open **Remote** on the device page's Device tab, or **Remote** in the Game Day app (**Settings > Device**, then **Advanced** on iPhone or **Remote** on Android).
2. Turn on **Discovery**. The status line says **Discovery on: press any button on the remote**.
3. Press any button on the WizMote.

The panel pairs with that remote and turns Discovery off by itself. The status line says **Paired:** and the remote's address. The press that pairs the remote does nothing else, so pairing with **OFF** leaves the panel on. Pressing the remote that is already paired while Discovery is on turns Discovery off and does nothing else either.

The panel pairs with one remote at a time. Pairing another one replaces the first. **Unpair remote** in the same section forgets the paired remote, and the status says **No remote paired**. A pairing or an unpair is saved the moment it happens, so it stays through a power cut right after. Discovery always starts off after the panel restarts, so a remote you haven't paired can't take over the panel.

## The buttons

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **ON** | Turns the panel on. |
| **OFF** | Turns the panel off. |
| Brighter | Raises the brightness by a tenth of full. On a panel that is off, it turns the panel on at the brightness it had. |
| Dimmer | Lowers the brightness by a tenth of full, down to the lowest setting. On a panel that is off, it does nothing, so pressing it in the dark can't leave the panel too dim to see next time. |
| **NIGHT** | Dims the panel right down to a night level, about 8% of full. On a panel that is off, it turns the panel on at that level. |
| **1** to **4** | Your first four favorite teams, until you set them to something else. See below. |

The brightness buttons move the same **Brightness** setting as the device page and the app.

During the [night schedule](/using/brightness-and-clock/)'s hours the panel stays at its night setting: the brightness buttons change the daytime brightness for the morning, and a screen that's off for the night stays off, whatever you press, until the hours end or a game of yours goes live. To light it up at night, turn **Night schedule** off under **Screen** on the device page's Display tab.

During the night schedule's hours the remote can always light the panel. **ON**, the brighter button and the numbered buttons turn night mode off for the rest of that night, at the panel's normal brightness. **NIGHT** does the same at the night level, about 8% of full, and pressing it again goes back to the schedule. Dimmer does nothing while night mode has the panel dimmed or dark, since it's already as dim as you set it. The next night the schedule runs as usual.

## The numbered buttons

Buttons 1 to 4 are the first four teams in your favorites list, in order. While the remote is paired, the device page marks them **remote button 1** to **4** under **Favorites** on the Teams tab. Each button also turns on a panel that is off.

- In **Favorite teams**, a button jumps to that favorite. If its game is on, the panel stays on it; otherwise its card shows for a moment and the panel carries on through your favorites.
- In **Live games**, a button switches the panel to Favorite teams and jumps to that favorite. A button set to **Live games** (below) switches it back.
- A button for an empty slot does nothing.

To change what a button does, reorder your favorites on the device page or in the app. See [Favorite teams](/using/favorites/).

**Set each button yourself.** **Remote** on the device page's Device tab, and Remote in the Game Day app, has a picker for each of buttons 1 to 4. A button starts on its own favorite (button 1 is favorite 1, and so on) and stays there until you change it; making a playlist no longer moves the buttons.

The picker lists your playlists first, then a group for each sport, with every league the panel follows: football (NFL and college), baseball, basketball (NBA, WNBA and men's college), hockey and soccer (MLS and Premier League). Under each sport, in this order:

- **All NFL live games** (one for each league in the sport): Live games in that league only. See [One league's live games](#one-leagues-live-games).
- **My football teams**: your favorites in that sport, the same as that sport's row under Playing. It's listed when you have two or more favorites in the sport and favorites in another sport too.
- Your favorites in that sport, each by name: that team, wherever it sits in your list.
- **Another NFL team...** (one for each league): opens a second list with every team in that league. A team that isn't one of your favorites goes on the button only. See [A team on a button only](#a-team-on-a-button-only).

The rest of the choices:

- **Its own favorite**: favorite 1 to 4, whichever team is in that slot. The default.
- A **playlist**, or **My football teams** and the other sport rows: the panel switches to Favorite teams, plays it and shows its name on the board for a moment, in the playlist's [color and icon](/using/favorites/#playlists) if you gave it one.
- **All favorites**: back to every favorite.
- **Live games**: back to Live games, with your own leagues, and **Live games** shows on the board for a moment. In Live games it leaves the panel as it is. With one button on Live games, a favorite's button pressed during Live games is no longer a one-way trip into Favorite teams.
- **Nothing**.
- A page under **Showing**: **Scoreboard**, **Clock**, **Smart board** or **Effects**. The button puts the panel on that page, the same as picking it under **Showing** on the device page. Pressing it while that page is already up leaves it there.
- One of your [effect playlists](/using/effects/#effect-playlists), listed under **Showing** by name, like **Effect playlist: Party**. See [A button that plays an effect playlist](#a-button-that-plays-an-effect-playlist).

A button set to a team, a playlist, a sport, a league, **All favorites** or **Live games** also brings the panel back to the scoreboard when another page is up, so the press always shows. If the [Clock](/using/brightness-and-clock/#showing-the-scoreboard-or-the-clock), the [Smart board](/using/smart-board/) or an [effect](/using/effects/) is up, the button switches **Showing** back to **Scoreboard** and shows what you pressed. If **After the game** has the clock or the Smart board up between games, the press ends that too: the panel stays on what you pressed, and the clock or Smart board comes back only after your next game's final. A button set to **Nothing** still does nothing.

### One league's live games

A button set to **All NFL live games** switches the panel to Live games and follows only NFL games, the same way Live games follows any league. **All NFL live games** shows on the board for a moment, so the press says what it picked. With no NFL game on, it shows what Live games shows between games: your first favorite's card, or recent NFL finals if you have no favorites. The leagues you picked for Live games stay as they are. The device page's Live games bar says **Remote button 2: NFL games only** while it's on.

It stays on through a restart, and ends when you pick a mode, a playlist or another button that picks what the panel follows. A page button (Scoreboard, Clock, Smart board, Effects, an effect playlist) leaves it on. A button set to **Live games** goes back to your own leagues.

### A team on a button only

Pick a team that isn't one of your favorites with **Another NFL team...** and the panel keeps it for that button only. It's saved with your favorites, so the panel can read its games, and it counts toward the 16 favorites the panel holds, but it's no favorite anywhere else: it stays out of All favorites, your playlists, the sport rows, Favorites take over and Game-day wake.

Press the button and the panel switches to Favorite teams and shows only that team: its game while it's on, its next or last game otherwise. Your other favorites don't take the panel over meanwhile. It stays on that team through a restart, and ends the same way as a league's live games.

**Favorites** on the Teams tab lists the team with **Button only** under its name. To make it a full favorite, tap it in the team picker (**Add a favorite**). When no button is set to it any more, it leaves your favorites by itself.

With 16 favorites already saved there's no room for another team: the picker says your favorites are full and leaves the button as it was. Remove a favorite first.

### A button with its own effect

A button set to **Effects** keeps an effect of its own: the effect, its palette, speed and intensity. Two buttons can hold two different effects, like PS Fire on 3 and Rain on 4. Pressing it puts that effect on and switches to **Effects**, so the effect from before doesn't flash up first.

When you pick **Effects** for a button, it saves what **Effects** is set to right then, and the picker shows it as **Effects:** with the effect and palette, like **Effects: Rain, Ocean**. To change it, tap **Change** under that button: it opens the button's own **Effect**, **Palette**, **Speed** and **Intensity**. Changing them changes only the button, not what the panel is playing now. Tap **Done** to close them.

### A button that plays an effect playlist

A button set to an effect playlist switches **Showing** to **Effects** and plays that playlist from its first effect, or from a random one when **Shuffle** is on. Pressing it again while the playlist plays starts it over. It's the same as tapping **Play** on the playlist: picking an effect, palette, speed or intensity yourself afterwards stops it and keeps what you picked, and so does anything that takes **Showing** off **Effects**, like a button set to a team.

Under **Effect playlists** on the Customize tab, a playlist a button plays shows **Button 2** (or whichever) beside its name. The picker lists only effect playlists that have effects in them.

A button set to a team you later remove, a playlist you delete or a sport you no longer have favorites in goes back to its own favorite for now, and **Remote** on the device page says so. So does a button on an effect playlist you delete or take every effect out of, and a button whose effect the panel's firmware doesn't have, after going back to an older version. Set it to something else, or bring the team or playlist back, and it works again. A button set to a playlist or an effect playlist follows it when you rename it. A settings backup carries the four buttons. A team on a button only comes back with its button when you restore one.
