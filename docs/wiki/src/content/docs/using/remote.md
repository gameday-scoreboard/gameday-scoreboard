The panel works with a WiZ WizMote, the small battery remote sold for WiZ smart bulbs. It talks to the panel directly, with no hub and no app, and works whether or not your phone is nearby.

## Pairing

1. Open the **Remote** card on the device page, or **Remote** in the Game Day app (**Settings > Device**, then **Advanced** on iPhone or **Remote** on Android).
2. Turn on **Discovery**. The status line says **Discovery on: press any button on the remote**.
3. Press any button on the WizMote.

The panel pairs with that remote and turns Discovery off by itself. The status line says **Paired:** and the remote's address.

The panel pairs with one remote at a time. Pairing another one replaces the first. **Unpair remote** in the same card forgets the paired remote, and the status says **No remote paired**. Discovery always starts off after the panel restarts, so a remote you haven't paired can't take over the panel.

## The buttons

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **ON** | Turns the panel on. |
| **OFF** | Turns the panel off. |
| Brighter | Raises the brightness by a tenth of full. On a panel that is off, it turns the panel on at the brightness it had. |
| Dimmer | Lowers the brightness by a tenth of full, down to the lowest setting. On a panel that is off, it does nothing, so pressing it in the dark can't leave the panel too dim to see next time. |
| **NIGHT** | Dims the panel right down to a night level, about 8% of full. On a panel that is off, it turns the panel on at that level. |
| **1** to **4** | Your first four favorite teams. See below. |

The brightness buttons move the same **Brightness** setting as the device page and the app.

During the [night schedule](/using/brightness-and-clock/)'s hours the panel stays at its night setting: the brightness buttons change the daytime brightness for the morning, and a screen that's off for the night stays off, whatever you press, until the hours end or a game of yours goes live. To light it up at night, turn **Night schedule** off in the Panel card on the device page.

:::note[Coming in the next release]
During the night schedule's hours the remote can always light the panel. **ON**, the brighter button and the numbered buttons turn night mode off for the rest of that night, at the panel's normal brightness. **NIGHT** does the same at the night level, about 8% of full, and pressing it again goes back to the schedule. Dimmer does nothing while night mode has the panel dimmed or dark, since it's already as dim as you set it. The next night the schedule runs as usual.
:::

## The numbered buttons

Buttons 1 to 4 are the first four teams in your favorites list, in order. The device page marks them in the Favorites card. Each button also turns on a panel that is off.

- In **Favorite teams**, a button jumps to that favorite. If its game is on, the panel stays on it; otherwise its card shows for a moment and the panel carries on through your favorites.
- In **Live games**, a button switches the panel to Favorite teams and jumps to that favorite.
- A button for an empty slot does nothing.

To change what a button does, reorder your favorites on the device page or in the app. See [Favorite teams](/using/favorites/).

With playlists, buttons 1 to 4 play your first four playlists instead of your first four favorites. A press turns the panel on, switches it to Favorite teams, plays that playlist and shows its name on the board for a moment. A button with no playlist does nothing. Reorder the playlists on the device page to change what each button plays. See [Playlists](/using/favorites/#playlists).

:::note[Coming in the next release]
You pick what each numbered button plays, and making a playlist no longer changes them. Under **Buttons 1 to 4** in the **Remote** card on the device page, each button has a list:

- **Favorite 1** (2, 3 or 4 for the other buttons): the team in that place in your favorites list. Every button starts here.
- One of your favorites by name. The button stays on that team when you reorder your favorites.
- One of your playlists. A press turns the panel on, switches it to Favorite teams, plays that playlist and shows its name on the board for a moment. The button stays on the playlist when you rename it or move it.
- A sport row, like **All football**, when your favorites cover more than one sport. A press plays every favorite in that sport, as picking the row under **Playing** does, and shows its name on the board for a moment.
- **All favorites**: switches the panel to Favorite teams with every favorite.
- **Nothing**: the button does nothing, and doesn't turn on a panel that is off.

A button stays on what you pick: adding, moving or renaming a favorite or a playlist never changes it. If you take out the team a button plays, remove its playlist, or its sport row goes (your favorites are down to one sport, or none in that sport), that button goes back to its own favorite, and the Remote card says so under it until you pick for that button again.

The Favorites card marks the teams a button plays, and the Playlists card marks the playlists. Your picks are in a settings backup.
:::
