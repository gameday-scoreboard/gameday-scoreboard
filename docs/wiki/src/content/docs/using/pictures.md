Between games the panel can show your own pictures: a fan photo, a piece of pixel art, your family's name in your team's colors. An animated GIF plays.

## Add a picture

1. Open the [device page](/using/device-page/) and find **Pictures**.
2. Tap **Add a picture** and pick a file.

The panel keeps up to 4 pictures, and they stay through a restart and through updates. Nothing shows until you add one.

What you can add:

- **A photo or any still picture** (PNG, JPG or WebP). The device page shrinks it to fit the panel, keeping all of it, and sends it as a PNG. A PNG that already fits goes as it is, so pixel art stays exact.
- **A GIF**, still or animated, up to 128 by 64 pixels and 64 KB, with up to 300 frames. The device page can't resize a GIF, so make it the right size first; any GIF editor can.

The panel checks every file before it keeps it, and says what's wrong with one it turns down, like a GIF that's too big.

### The right size

| Your panel | Picture size |
| --- | --- |
| One 64x64 panel | 64 by 64 pixels |
| Two panels side by side | 128 by 64 pixels |

A picture smaller than the screen is shown in the middle, on black. A 128-wide picture on a single 64x64 panel is shown at half size, so all of it still fits. Transparent parts show black.

## When it shows

Every 3 minutes between games, the next picture takes the screen for 10 seconds. An animated GIF plays at its own speed for about 10 seconds, or once through if it's longer, up to 30 seconds. A picture you just added comes up next.

The picture card works like the [message card](/using/messages/#a-message-card):

- It never covers a live game, a celebration, or the panel's own trouble messages, like **No internet**.
- It takes turns with the message card, the [news](/using/news/) and the [College Top 25](/using/top-25/): only one is on the screen at a time.

## Delete a picture

Tap **Delete** next to it under **Pictures**. With no pictures left, the card is off.

## Room on the panel

Pictures live in the same memory as the panel's settings. The panel always keeps room for its settings, so on a panel with a lot saved it may take fewer or smaller pictures; the device page says so when one doesn't fit.
