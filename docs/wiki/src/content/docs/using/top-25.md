While you follow a college team, the panel shows this week's AP Top 25 between games: each team's rank, its abbreviation and its record, a page at a time.

## When it shows

The Top 25 shows when the panel follows a college football or men's college basketball team:

- **Favorite teams**: one of your favorites is a college football or basketball team.
- **Live games**: college football or college basketball is one of the leagues you picked, or one of your favorites is a college team.

A panel with no team picked yet doesn't show it.

It takes a turn every 10 minutes while no game is on, over whatever the board shows: the next game, a countdown, the off-season clock or the final scores. A turn goes through all 25 teams, five seconds a page, then the board comes back. It never covers a game in progress, a score splash or the line the panel shows when it can't reach ESPN, and it waits for your [message card](/using/messages/) to finish when both are due. A message card on **Whenever no game is on** keeps it off.

If you follow both sports, football and basketball take turns. A poll more than 50 days old is left off, so football's stays up through the bowl games until the final poll after the title game, and basketball's shows from the preseason poll in October to the final one in April.

## What it looks like

The top line names the poll, like **CFB AP WK 9** for college football's week 9 poll, **CBB AP PRE** for basketball's preseason poll, or **CFB AP FINAL** for the last poll of the season. Each row below has the rank, the team and its record:

```
 1 MIA  7-0
 2 OSU  6-1
 3 UGA  6-1
```

Before a team has played, as in the preseason poll, the record is left off. Teams tied in the poll share a rank.

A single 64x64 panel shows five teams a page, so a turn is five pages. A 128x64 display shows ten teams a page in two columns, so a turn is three pages.

## Turning it off

The Top 25 is on for every panel. Turn it off with **College Top 25** under **Between games** on the [device page](/using/device-page/). A ranked team's rank on the corner of its logo on the [game board](/using/football/#before-the-game) is always drawn; this switch doesn't change it.

## Where it comes from

The panel reads the AP poll from ESPN once a day for each sport it follows, about 28 KB each time, and keeps it until the next read. If a read fails it tries again half an hour later and keeps showing the poll it has.
