Between games the panel counts down to the next one in big digits, and it can count down to a day you pick.

## The countdown to the next game

While your team's next game is still to come, the board takes turns: the next game's board with both logos for 10 seconds, then the countdown for 10 seconds. The countdown is in your team's colors, with the matchup under it, like "DAL vs PHI" at home or "DAL at PHI" away, and the ticker keeps running along the bottom.

- A day or more out it reads days and hours, like **2d 14h**.
- Under a day it reads hours and minutes, like **14h 05m**, then minutes alone in the last hour, like **45m**.
- At kickoff it stops and the board stays on the game.

It works the same way in each mode:

- **My team**: your team's next game, every 10 seconds.
- **Favorite teams**: each favorite's board in the playlist gets a second turn for its countdown before the playlist moves on. Once a game takes the panel shortly before kickoff, the countdown stops.
- **Live games** with a team picked and nothing live: your team's card, the same as My team.

Between seasons, when the board shows the [off-season clock](/using/brightness-and-clock/#the-off-season-clock) and the next game is on the schedule, the clock and the countdown take turns, so the panel counts down to opening night on its own.

A game with no start time yet, shown as TBD, gets no countdown.

The countdown is on for every panel. Turn it off with **Countdown to the next game** under **Between games** on the [device page](/using/device-page/).

## Your own countdown

Under **Between games** on the device page, give a countdown a **Name**, like "Christmas" or "Opening day", and a **Day**. The panel counts down to the start of that day in the panel's timezone, and on the day itself it reads **Today**. The day after, it stops showing; set a new day for the next one. **Clear the countdown** removes it.

It takes the last 10 seconds of every minute, whenever no game is on, in every mode: over the next game's board, the off-season clock, the Favorite teams playlist, and the Later today and final scores in Live games. A game in progress, or a favorite's game that has taken the panel, is never covered.

The name can be up to 20 characters long, fewer with accented letters. Emoji are left out, since the panel can't draw them. Without a name, the panel shows the day, like "Dec 25". It's in your team's colors, or your favorite's in Favorite teams. When Live games is showing other teams' games, or no team is picked, it's gray.

## On a 64x64 panel

A single 64x64 panel shows the countdown in the same size as the off-season clock's time, with the matchup or the name under it.
