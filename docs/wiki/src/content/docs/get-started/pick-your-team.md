Your teams are your favorites: one team, or up to 16 from any league. Pick them in the Game Day app or on the panel's device page. Both change the same list, so use whichever is closer to hand.

Favorites are the only way to pick a team.

## From the Game Day app

Once the app has put the panel on your Wi-Fi, it opens the panel so you can add your teams. Later, open the **Teams** tab and tap **Add a favorite** to add more. The app's team chooser has the same league tabs, search and **On now** tab as the device page's. See [The Game Day app](/get-started/the-app/).

## From the device page

1. Open the panel's address in a browser on any phone or computer on the same Wi-Fi. It's the `gameday-xxxxxx.local` name the panel shows when it starts, with your panel's own six characters, like `gameday-2f6a70.local`. If your phone won't open a `.local` address, use the IP address the panel shows instead.
2. Make sure **Favorite teams** is on, the first of the two buttons under the board.
3. In the **Favorites** card, tap **Add a favorite**.
4. Pick a league tab and tap a logo, or type in **Search teams** to find a team in any league. Add more if you like, then tap **Done**.

The page says **Added the** and the team's name, and the panel switches to it within a few seconds.

Lost the address? Hold the button on the controller for about 1.5 seconds and the panel shows it again, with the IP address, for 20 seconds.

## The leagues

| League | Teams you can pick |
| --- | --- |
| NFL | All 32 teams |
| College football (NCAAF) | Every FBS team |
| MLB | Every team |
| NBA | Every team |
| WNBA | Every team |
| Men's college basketball (NCAAM) | Every Division I team |
| NHL | Every team |
| MLS | Every team |
| Premier League (EPL) | Every team |

Soccer clubs are followed into their cup competitions too: Premier League clubs into the Champions League, Europa League and Conference League, and MLS clubs into the CONCACAF Champions Cup.

Each sport has its own board. See [Football](/using/football/), [Baseball](/using/baseball/), [Basketball](/using/basketball/), [Hockey](/using/hockey/) and [Soccer](/using/soccer/).

## The On now tab

The first tab in the team picker, **On now**, lists today's games across the leagues, straight from ESPN. They're grouped as **Live now**, **Later today**, **Final** and **Postponed or canceled**. Tap the side you want to follow to add that team.

Your browser reads this list from ESPN, so the tab needs your phone or computer to be online, not only the panel.

:::note[Coming in the next release]
Search on **On now** finds a team that isn't playing today. When nothing on today's list matches what you typed, the teams that do match show under a **Teams** heading, with their league, and a tap adds one. Today's games still come first when one matches.
:::

## Changes take effect right away

A new team shows on the panel within a few seconds, with nothing to restart. The panel keeps your teams, and every other setting, through restarts and power cuts.

## Before you pick a team

A panel with no favorites doesn't follow a team. It shows live games, and when nothing is on, the latest final scores and when the next game starts. The app's QR code comes back over the board for 13 seconds every 3 minutes until you pick a team.

Those live games come from every league the panel supports, hockey, basketball, baseball and soccer as well as football, and the QR code says **Pick your team in the app**. See [A panel with no favorites](/using/modes/#a-panel-with-no-favorites).

## More than one team

**Favorite teams** cycles through your teams in your order, and a team's game takes the panel shortly before kickoff. The device page has one other mode:

- **Live games** follows a game in progress in the leagues you choose and moves on when it ends.

See [Modes](/using/modes/) and [Favorite teams](/using/favorites/).
