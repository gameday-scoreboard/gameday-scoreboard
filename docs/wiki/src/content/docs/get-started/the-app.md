The Game Day app is the companion app for your panel, for iPhone and Android. It has no account and no cloud service. The app talks to the panel directly, over Bluetooth during setup and over your Wi-Fi after that.

## What the app does

- **Finds your panels.** A new panel shows up as a new panel nearby, over Bluetooth. A panel that's already on your Wi-Fi shows up under **Found on your network**.
- **Sets a new panel up over Bluetooth.** The panel sends the app the Wi-Fi networks it can see, strongest first. You pick yours and enter the password. See [Set up your panel](/get-started/setup/).
- **Picks your teams** and the mode, the same choices as the device page. See [Pick your team](/get-started/pick-your-team/).
- **Changes the panel's settings**: the ticker, celebrations, favorites, brightness, the remote and the rest. They are the same settings as on the device page, and a change in one shows in the other.

## Board, Teams and Settings

Both apps have three tabs: **Board**, **Teams** and **Settings**. They show the panel you picked last. The iPhone app runs on iPad too, with the same tabs.

A few things are on iPhone only for now: the night schedule, and the **Pick the best game** and **Favorites take over** switches. They're marked below. On Android, use the [device page](/using/device-page/) for them.

:::note[Coming in the next app update]
The Android app gets all three, so none of them stays on iPhone only:

- **Settings > Display > Screen** has the night schedule: **Night schedule**, **Starts at**, **Ends at**, **Screen at night** and **Night brightness**. See [Night schedule](/using/brightness-and-clock/#night-schedule).
- The Board's night line below, with **Turn on for tonight** and **Back to night mode**, is on Android too.
- **Teams > Live games** has **Pick the best game** under **Switch every**: on, the panel follows the closest game with the least time left and moves only at a break in play; off, a random game. The Board's line says "Showing the best game on" while it's on. It shows once the panel runs firmware with the switch. See [Live games](/using/modes/#live-games).
- **Teams > Live games** has **Favorites take over**, with **Two favorites at once** while it's on.
:::

### Board

What you reach for during a game:

- The game on the panel, and what's up next.
- The mode, **Favorites** or **Live games**, with the one switch that goes with it: **Only today's games** for Favorites, the leagues for Live games. The device page calls the first one **Favorite teams**.
- **Message**, for a banner or a message card on the panel. See [Your own messages](/using/messages/).
- **Brightness**, **Screen** to turn the panel's screen off and on, and **Refresh now** to read the scores again right away.

The panel's name is at the top. Tap it to switch to another panel, to add one, or, with two or more, to manage them.

In both apps the Board follows the device page's Home tab:

- The modes read **Favorite teams** ("Only the teams you pick") and **Live games** ("Games on right now, in leagues you pick"). The line under them says why the panel shows what it does, like "None of your teams are playing. Showing their next games." With no favorites it offers **Add a team**. On iPhone, with playlists, it has **Playing**; on Android **Playing** stays in **Teams > Favorites**.
- **Panel on**, **Brightness** and **Showing** sit together, with **Refresh scores**. When **Showing** is on **Effects**, the effect, palette, speed and intensity show under it.
- **Customize** takes the place of **Message**. On Android it's in **Settings**, next to **Display** and **Device**.

:::note[Coming in the next app update]
When **Showing** is on **Effects** and the panel has [effect playlists](/using/effects/#effect-playlists), **Effect playlist** shows under the effect controls, as on the device page's Home tab. Pick one to play it from the top, or **None** to stop it. While one plays, the picker names it. Picking an effect, palette, speed or intensity yourself stops it, and the panel keeps your pick. You make and change effect playlists on the device page's **Customize** tab.
:::

:::note[Coming in the next app update]
On Android, when **Showing** is on **Weather**, the line under it says the page stays up until you pick another one and names the place its forecast is for. With no town set, it asks you to set your town first, in **Display > Weather**. It shows once the panel runs firmware with the Weather page.
:::

If the app loses its connection to the panel for a moment while the panel still answers, **Reconnecting…** shows at the top of the Board until it's back. If the panel doesn't answer at all, the Board says **Can't reach** and the panel's name, with what to check.

When the panel shows its thermometer or plug in the corner, the Board shows the same words under the game, **Running warm** or **Power dipped**, with what to do. There's nothing to press: it goes when the panel's icon does. See [Icons in the corner](/help/panel-messages/#icons-in-the-corner).

On iPhone, while the night schedule has the panel dimmed or dark, the Board says so under the game, like **Night mode until 7:00 AM**, with what brings the panel back. See [Night schedule](/using/brightness-and-clock/#night-schedule).

:::note[Coming in the next app update]
Under **Night mode until**, **Turn on for tonight** puts the panel back at its normal brightness for the rest of the night. Turning **Panel on** back on during the night does the same. The Board then says **Night mode off until** the next start time, like **Night mode off until 11:00 PM**, and **Back to night mode** goes back to the schedule. During a live game of yours there's no button, since the game already has the panel lit. The next night the schedule runs as usual.
:::

## Your panels

The panel list is **Settings > Panels**, and it's what the Board shows until you have a panel. It shows each panel's nickname, with a dot for online and **Offline** next to a panel the app can't reach.

:::note[Coming in the next app update]
On iPhone and Android:

- When the app can't reach any of your panels at once, the rows say **Reconnecting** and the app tries again every few seconds. A phone that was asleep can take a moment to find your panels on the network, even though they're fine. If none answers for three minutes, the rows say **Offline** and the list suggests turning Wi-Fi off and on (in Control Center on iPhone, in Quick Settings on Android).
- The dots start fresh each time you open the app, so you never see one left over from earlier.
- A panel you give a new name in its own config stays in your list under the new name, with its nickname.
:::

- **Nickname**: give each panel a name like "Living room", in **Settings > Device > Nickname**. The nickname is saved on the panel, so every phone and the device page see the same name.
- **Order**: with two or more panels, put the list in your own order (**Edit** on iPhone, the handle on each row on Android). The order is kept on each phone.

## The Live score widget

Both apps have a **Live score** widget for your Home Screen with your panel's game: the teams, the score, and the clock or the start time. Between games it shows the panel's next game, or what the panel says when there's nothing on. Tap it to open the app on that panel.

- **iPhone**: a small and a medium size for the Home Screen, and two for the Lock Screen. With more than one panel, touch and hold the widget, choose **Edit Widget** and pick the **Panel**.
- **Android**: a 2 by 2 widget, and a wider look when you stretch it to 4 by 2. With more than one panel, the app asks which one when you place it. To change it later, touch and hold the widget and open its settings (Android 12 and newer).

The widget reads your panel over your home Wi-Fi: about every 10 minutes while a game is on, less often between games, and straight away while the app is open on that panel. Away from home it keeps the last score for a few minutes, then says it can't reach the panel. Open the app on your home Wi-Fi and it catches up.

## Try the app before your panel arrives

With no panel saved yet, the panel list has **Try a demo panel**. It opens a demo panel inside the app, with made-up teams playing a game on a loop, so you can look around every tab. Nothing you change there reaches a real panel, and the demo isn't saved to your list. To leave it, tap its name at the top of the Board and choose **Close the demo**.

## If the app doesn't list your panel

Add it by address. On the panel list, tap **+** and enter the panel's name, like `gameday-2f6a70.local`, or its IP address, like `192.168.1.50`. Both show on the panel when it starts. To see them again, hold the button on the controller for about 1.5 seconds.

The phone has to be on the same Wi-Fi network as the panel, or on a network your router lets reach it, like a main network reaching an [IoT network or VLAN](/using/wifi/#iot-network-iot-vlan-or-iot-subnet). A guest network can keep the two apart. More in [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting/).

## The QR code on the panel

The QR code the panel shows during setup, when it has lost Wi-Fi and right after it joins, opens `https://app.gameday-scoreboard.com/setup`. On an iPhone, point the Camera at it. It opens the Game Day app if you have it installed, and the setup page in your browser if you don't.

## Without the app

You don't need the app to use the panel.

- **To put it on Wi-Fi**, join the panel's own hotspot from any phone or laptop. It's named **Game Day** plus six letters and numbers, like `Game Day 2f6a70`, and a setup page opens when you join it. See [Set up your panel](/get-started/setup/#without-the-app-use-the-panels-hotspot).
- **To pick your team and change settings**, open the panel's device page in any browser on the same Wi-Fi, at the `gameday-xxxxxx.local` name or IP address the panel shows. Everything the app sets is there. See [The device page](/using/device-page/).
